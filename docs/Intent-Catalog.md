# DuoConvo Intent Catalog

## Overview

An intent represents the underlying meaning or purpose of a user's utterance. The AI model learns to recognise these intents through semantic understanding rather than exact keyword matching.

Each intent contains one or more canonical concepts. During training, multiple paraphrases of the same concept are linked together so the model learns that different wordings can express the same meaning.

---

# 1. Commerce

## PRICE_INQUIRY
Asking about the price of an item.

Examples:
- How much is this?
- What's the price?
- How much does this cost?

---

## NEGOTIATION
Requesting a discount or negotiating a better price.

Examples:
- Can you reduce the price?
- That's too expensive.
- Give me your best price.

---

## PRODUCT_AVAILABILITY
Checking whether a product is available.

Examples:
- Do you have tomatoes?
- Is this available?
- Do you sell rice?

---

## QUANTITY_REQUEST
Asking for a specific quantity.

Examples:
- I need two kilograms.
- Give me five oranges.
- Half a basket please.

---

## PAYMENT
Conversations relating to payment.

Examples:
- I want to pay.
- Do you accept Mobile Money?
- Can I pay by cash?
